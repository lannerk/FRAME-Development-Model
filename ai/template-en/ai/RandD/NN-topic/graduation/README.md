# Graduation — eight steps of review, six documents

> 🔴 **Review is not reading `log/` end to end.** You walk the indexes in eight steps and **open only the rounds that are referenced**;
> the test: after the eight steps you can write the graduation output **without reading most of the body of `log/`**. If you cannot, the indexes were not kept — a debt from every earlier round.

## The eight steps

1. `02-ideas.md` — what he wants → 2. `decisions.md` — what was settled and what was rejected → 3. `tasks/index.md` — what was chased, 🔴 **and what cannot be done**
→ 4. `notes/INDEX.md` — what the world looks like → 5. `lab/INDEX.md` `runs/INDEX.md` — which judgements have an experiment behind them
→ 6. `drafts/` — how we claim it should be done → 7. 🔴 **open only the referenced rounds of `log/`** → 8. reconcile against `00-status.md`
